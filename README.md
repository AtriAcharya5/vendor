📌 Project Overview
This repository features a comprehensive, interactive data analytics solution designed to track, monitor, and assess vendor sales performance and inventory health. Utilizing a robust suite of supply chain and retail metrics, the dashboard provides the executive team with high-level insights into vendor performance, sales volume vs. purchasing costs, and product health status to optimize procurement and revenue management strategies.

🎯 Purpose & Objectives
The primary goal of this project is to parse critical retail metrics to uncover operational patterns and financial profitability margins across various vendors and specific product descriptions. Additionally, the dashboard cross-references on-hand inventory volume with total sales performance to ensure procurement pipelines run efficiently without overstocking or stockouts.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies UsedPower BI Desktop 
– For data modeling, visualization design, and report building.
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – To create complex dynamic measures and time-intelligence KPIs (e.g., MTD and MoM calculations).
Power Query / ETL – For data transformation, data cleaning, and schema optimization.

.📊 Core DAX & Metrics 
ImplementedThe project leverages precise data operations to isolate specific supply chain and profitability behaviors:Profit Margin Analysis: Dynamic aggregation of Sum of Profit broken down by unique vendor distributions to isolate high-yield partnerships.Categorical Performance Flags: Structural integration of product tracking metrics, including Brand_Performance_Status (e.g., highlighting items safely categorized under "Healthy" inventory statuses).Procurement Efficiency Check: Relational tracking measures contrasting the exact cost of inventory intake (Purchases_Dollar_sum) against final retail volume values (Sum of SalesPrice).

.🏁 ConclusionThe dashboard successfully provides a clear, data-driven window into the retail supply chain ecosystem. While top-tier mainstream brands like Diageo and Smirnoff comfortably secure the highest volume and top-line sales visibility, the granular profit analysis reveals that niche vendors hold remarkably high margins and generate vital percentages of actual net profit. This architecture empowers purchasing departments to align seasonal purchase orders with precise brand velocities and rebalance capital allocations between high-volume staples and high-profit niche brands.
